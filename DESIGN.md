# MGBC Visual Direction

## Product mode
Premium Saudi financial consultancy marketing site with a conversion-focused consultation funnel.

## Audience
Founders, finance leaders, investors, and business owners who need practical financial, tax, or feasibility guidance.

## Visual world
Confident and precise rather than luxurious for its own sake. Navy represents clarity and trust; gold is reserved for decisions and actions. The upward arrow inside the MGBC mark is the single signature device.

## Composition
Editorial whitespace, strong Arabic type hierarchy, mostly flat sections, limited cards, and one immersive navy hero. Avoid generic SaaS gradients, floating glass cards, excessive rounded containers, and decorative metrics without evidence.

## References
- Nitaj: service discoverability and conversion flow.
- Grant Thornton Saudi Arabia: authority and insight architecture.
- AstroWind: Astro project structure, SEO, content collections, and static performance.
- Plax: restrained motion and spacious composition only.
